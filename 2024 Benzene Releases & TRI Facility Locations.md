# 2024 Benzene Releases & TRI Facility Locations in Los Angeles County, California

## Project Overview
This project uses ArcGIS Pro to analyze and visualize the geographic distribution of benzene releases reported by facilities in Los Angeles County, California, in 2024. Using data from the EPA Toxic Release Inventory (TRI), the project identifies facilities reporting onsite benzene releases and examines how release amounts are distributed across geographic areas.

## Tools & Technologies
- **ArcGIS Pro:** Geographic mapping, spatial visualization, and thematic mapping.
- **Microsoft Excel:** Data cleaning, filtering, and calculating total onsite releases by facility.
- **EPA Toxic Release Inventory (TRI):** Environmental facility and chemical release data.

## Methodology
- Started with the 2024 California TRI dataset containing 3,361 records and 100 attributes.
- Filtered the dataset to identify benzene-related releases in Los Angeles County, resulting in approximately 22 facilities.
- Used Excel to calculate total onsite benzene releases for each facility.
- Imported facility coordinates and census tract boundaries into ArcGIS Pro.
- Created a thematic map using graduated colors and proportional symbols to visualize release amounts and facility locations.

## Key Findings
- Benzene-releasing facilities were unevenly distributed throughout Los Angeles County.
- Noticeable concentrations appeared in central and southern Los Angeles County, particularly around major urban and industrial areas, including the port region.
- Larger, darker red symbols identified facilities reporting more than 50,000 pounds of onsite benzene releases.

## Project Output
A thematic GIS map illustrating benzene release amounts across Los Angeles County census tracts and the locations of facilities reporting onsite benzene releases in 2024.
